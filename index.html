Frequently asked questions

What business owners need to know.

Does TallyVoice replace QuickBooks or Xero?

No. TallyVoice sits above your accounting platform and helps you understand the information already stored there.

Does TallyVoice give tax advice?

No. It provides estimates, highlights missing information and helps prepare questions for your accountant or bookkeeper.

Will TallyVoice send messages or make payments automatically?

Sensitive actions require your approval. You stay in control of reminders, reports and payment-related workflows.

Is it only for Australian businesses?

The initial product focuses on Australian SME language, GST, BAS and local business workflows. The platform is designed to support future markets.
